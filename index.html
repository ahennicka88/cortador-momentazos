Cortador Momentazos

A personal desktop tool that turns long live-stream recordings into short vertical clips for the Momentazos channels.

What it does

  • Finds short moments that make sense on their own in recordings of public live streams, and cuts them into vertical clips with captions and credit to the streamer.
  • The channel owner reviews and approves every clip by hand before anything is sent.
  • Sends approved clips only to the owner's own accounts: to the TikTok inbox as a draft, as a YouTube Short and as an Instagram Reel.

How it uses TikTok

The owner connects their own TikTok account with TikTok Login Kit for desktop. The app then uses the Content Posting API (upload) to send each approved clip to that account's inbox. The clip arrives as a draft: the owner opens TikTok, adds the caption and sound, and publishes it there. The app does not post on its own and does not read any other TikTok data.

Who can use it

Only the owner of the Momentazos accounts (@momentazos77). The app runs on the owner's own computer and is not offered for download or to other users.
