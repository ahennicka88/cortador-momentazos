Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

This policy explains what Cortador Momentazos ("the app") does with data. The app is a personal tool used only by the owner of the Momentazos accounts, @momentazos77 ("the owner"), on the owner's own computer.

Summary

  • The app has one user: the owner. It has no sign-up, no ads and no analytics.
  • It keeps the keys that let it upload to the owner's accounts, on the owner's computer only.
  • It never sells or shares personal data.

Data from TikTok

When the owner connects their TikTok account with TikTok Login Kit, the app asks for two permissions:

  • user.info.basic: TikTok returns the account's identifier (open_id) with the login.
  • video.upload: lets the app send the owner's clips to the account's inbox as drafts.

The app receives an access token, a refresh token and the open_id. It uses them only to upload the owner's clips and to check that each upload finished. It does not read videos, followers, comments, messages or any data about other TikTok users.

Data from YouTube and Instagram

  • YouTube: the app asks only for the youtube.upload permission, to upload the owner's clips as Shorts. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Instagram: the app uses the instagram_basic and instagram_content_publish permissions to publish Reels to the owner's professional account.

Where data is kept

Tokens are saved in a private folder on the owner's computer. They are sent only to the official API of the platform they belong to (TikTok, Google or Meta), over HTTPS, and are never written to logs.

Video processing

To find moments and write captions, the app processes recordings of public live streams. Speech recognition runs on the owner's computer or in the owner's own cloud notebook (Kaggle), and an AI text model (NVIDIA) reads the transcript to suggest moments. These services receive stream audio or transcripts only, never TikTok, YouTube or Instagram account data.

Sharing

Data is not sold, rented or shared with anyone else, and is not used for advertising.

Keeping and deleting data

Tokens are kept until the owner disconnects the account. Access can be removed at any time:

  • TikTok: in the TikTok app, open Settings and privacy and remove Cortador Momentazos from the apps and services connected to the account.
  • Google: at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Instagram: in the Instagram or Meta Business settings, under apps and websites.

Deleting the keys saved by the app also stops all access from the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly process data about children.

Changes

This policy may be updated. The date at the top shows the current version.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, send a direct message to @momentazos77 on TikTok or Instagram.

Cortador Momentazos is an independent project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by TikTok, YouTube or Instagram.
